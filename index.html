A small recording ritual.

  1. Settle in. Enable your mic, and camera if you like. Use headphones when listening to takes.
  2. One chapter at a time. A three-second countdown gives you a breath. The clock shows how much time is left. Re-record freely; every take stays available.
  3. Choose & trim. Choose a take for each chapter. Trim pauses at the edges, or add a short lead-in. Check your camera does not hide important evidence.
  4. Bring it together. Build a preview, then export the full video with your voice and optional presenter overlay. Levels and tiny fades smooth audio cuts; mismatched words need a retake.
  5. Keep a copy. Download the final video and a project backup. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted.

Use a current Chrome or Edge browser on your Mac for the best recording and export support. Camera availability comes from macOS; the studio cannot enable an unavailable iPhone remotely.
